## Overview

DeadBro is a Rails APM with request-based pricing offering live traces, slow SQL detection, N+1 detection, error tracking, and uptime checks.

## Features

- Live traces
- Slow SQL detection
- N+1 detection
- Error tracking
- 1 uptime check
- Email alerts
- 50k requests/month (free forever)
- 7-day retention
- 1 app

## Pricing

Free forever for 50k requests/month, 7-day retention, 1 app, 1 uptime check, and email alerts. No credit card required.
