## Overview

whatbroke.today is an AI-powered outage aggregator that monitors 100+ cloud services and sends alerts when they go down.

## Key Features

- Monitors AWS, Vercel, Stripe, GitHub, Cloudflare, and 100+ services
- AI-generated incident summaries
- Real-time Telegram alerts (@whatbroketoday)
- RSS feed for DevOps workflows

Free to use.
