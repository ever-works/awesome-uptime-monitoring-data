## Overview

Beeceptor is a no-code, cloud-based platform for mocking and debugging multi-protocol APIs.

## Features

- Instant mock servers for REST, SOAP, gRPC & GraphQL
- Rules-based logic and CRUD & stateful mocking
- Proxying and CORS management
- Public dashboard/endpoint for viewing submitted requests and responses

## Pricing

Free plan includes 50 requests per day and a public dashboard/endpoint where anyone with the dashboard URL can view submitted requests and responses.
