## Overview

SigNoz is an open-source observability platform designed for cloud-native applications. It unifies metrics, traces, and logs into a single platform for monitoring and troubleshooting microservices architectures.

## Features

- OpenTelemetry-native observability platform
- Metrics, traces, and logs in a single pane
- Distributed tracing with automatic instrumentation
- Log management and querying
- Real-time metrics visualization
- Dashboards and alerts
- Self-hosted deployment
