## Overview

RealUptime provides uptime checks from 4 regions every 3 minutes with a public status page and API/CLI/Terraform/MCP access.

## Features

- Uptime checks from 4 regions
- 3-minute check intervals
- Public status page with 100 subscribers
- API, CLI, Terraform and MCP access
- 10 monitors (free)
- Commercial use allowed

## Pricing

Free for 10 monitors, commercial use allowed, no card required.
