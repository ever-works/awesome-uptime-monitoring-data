## Overview

CertAvert monitors SSL/TLS certificates on any port, checking expiry, chain problems, revocation, and host mismatch.

## Features

- Monitor SSL/TLS certificates on any port
- Check certificate expiry
- Check chain problems
- Check revocation status
- Check host mismatch
- Expiry notifications via email, Slack, or Discord

## Pricing

Free tier: Monitor 15 domains free with expiry notifications via email, Slack or Discord. No credit card required for signup.
