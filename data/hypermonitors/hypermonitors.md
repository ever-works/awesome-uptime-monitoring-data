## Overview

Hypermonitors monitors infrastructure from three different locations (ZH - NYC - SG) with every outage checked from a 2nd location before alerts go out.

## Features

- Multi-location monitoring (ZH, NYC, SG)
- Outage verification from 2nd location
- 5 monitors (free forever)
- 5 minute checks

## Pricing

Free forever for 5 monitors and 5 minute checks.
