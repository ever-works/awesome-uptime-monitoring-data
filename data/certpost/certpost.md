## Overview

CertPost provides live SSL/TLS certificate monitoring on port 443 or custom ports (SMTP/IMAP). It reads the served certificate on the wire, performs full chain verification, and sends alerts via email or webhook before expiration.

## Features

- Live SSL/TLS certificate monitoring on port 443 or custom ports
- Reads served certificate on the wire
- Full chain verification
- Alerts via email or webhook before expiration

## Pricing

Free tier: 3 certificates monitored forever.
