## Overview

AIWatch is an open-source dashboard for monitoring third-party AI services.

## Features

- Monitoring for 31 AI services including Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral, and Groq
- Reliability scoring
- Incident analysis
- Discord and Slack alerts
- Built on Cloudflare Workers + KV
- Live demo available at ai-watch.dev

## Pricing
