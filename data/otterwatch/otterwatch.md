## Overview

Otterwatch provides daily SSL/TLS certificate monitoring including expiry alerts (30/7/1 day), chain and OCSP revocation checks, and certificate transparency issuance history.

## Features

- Daily SSL/TLS certificate monitoring
- Expiry alerts at 30/7/1 day intervals
- Chain verification
- OCSP revocation checks
- Certificate transparency issuance history

Free forever plan: monitoring for 5 domains, no credit card required.
