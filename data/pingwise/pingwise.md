## Overview

PingWise is a quorum-based uptime monitoring service using 15 probes in 6 different continents.

## Features

- Quorum-based monitoring
- 15 probes in 6 different continents
- Credit-based pricing
- 650,000 free checks per month (75 free monitors at 5-minute checks)
- No credit card required
- Unlimited users

## Pricing

Credit-based pricing with 650,000 free checks per month.
