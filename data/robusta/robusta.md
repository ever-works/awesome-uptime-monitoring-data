## Overview

Robusta is powerful Kubernetes monitoring based on Prometheus.

## Features

- Kubernetes monitoring
- Prometheus-based
- Bring your own Prometheus or all-in-one bundle
- Up to 20 Kubernetes nodes (free tier)
- Alerts via Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord
- Integrations with PagerDuty, OpsGenie, VictorOps, DataDog

## Pricing

Free tier includes up to 20 Kubernetes nodes.
